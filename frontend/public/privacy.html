Privacy Policy

Automated Email AI · Last updated 2 September 2026

Automated Email AI ("the app") is an AI email assistant. This policy explains what it collects, why, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What the app collects

  • Account identity. Your name, email address, and profile picture, from Google if you sign in with Google, or just your email address if you sign up with a password.
  • Your conversations. Messages you exchange with the assistant, so the app can show you your history.
  • Gmail data — only if you choose to link Gmail. Message headers, subjects, snippets and bodies are read from your mailbox at the moment you open a feature that needs them. Signing in does not link Gmail; linking is a separate, explicit step.

The Gmail permissions, and what each is for

  • gmail.readonly — reading your inbox to produce summaries, analytics, contact lists and notifications.
  • gmail.send — sending an email, only when you press send on a message you have reviewed. The app never sends mail on its own; even in Agent Mode a drafted email is handed back to you for approval.
  • gmail.modify — archiving, starring, labelling, trashing or marking messages read, only in response to an action you take in the interface.
Google Limited Use disclosure. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Gmail data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train any AI model, and never read by a human.

Where it goes

Accounts, conversations and your Google access token are stored in a Supabase Postgres database, protected by row-level security so one account cannot read another's rows. The app runs on Vercel and Render.

Email content is sent to Mistral AI to generate summaries, replies and drafts. It is transmitted for that request and is not used to train their models. Account verification codes are delivered by Brevo. No other party receives your data, and nothing is ever sold.

What is not stored

Your emails are not copied into the app's database. They are fetched from Gmail when a screen needs them and kept only for as long as that request takes. The app never sees your Google password.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect Gmail: revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored token stops working immediately.
  • Delete everything: email rehanvhora86@gmail.com from your account address and the account, its conversations and its stored token will be deleted within 30 days.

A note on what this project is

This is an independently built portfolio project, offered for demonstration and evaluation. It is not a commercial service and has no employees, no analytics trackers and no advertising.
